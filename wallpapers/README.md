Nine 3840 × 2160 JPEG wallpapers for a widescreen TV. Download individual images below, or get the wallpaper ZIP from the [Flick TV releases](https://github.com/alphasixtyfive/flick-tv/releases).

Copy the pictures to storage available on your TV. In Flick, open **Settings → Wallpapers → Choose photos** to add them to the shared slideshow. To give one profile a fixed picture, open **Settings → Profiles**, edit that profile and choose **Profile wallpaper**. Android's photo picker grants Flick access to the selected image.
